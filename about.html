Why we wholesale

Boutique taste, reseller prices

Her Accessori Wholesale grew out of the Her Accessori boutique, founded by Cynthia Wambui Kimani. Sourcing delicate, well-chosen pieces for our own shelves taught us something: small shop owners want the same taste, at prices that leave room to resell.

So we opened the supply side. We source jewelry and perfume in dozens and bulk packs, price them for resale, and deliver them straight to small shops. You focus on your customers. We keep your shelves stocked.

Every lot is chosen with the same eye the boutique is known for. If it isn't good enough for our shelves, it doesn't reach yours.
